# 01 - Prompt basics: client meeting brief

Turn an account manager's scattered notes into a one-page brief before a client meeting.
This folder teaches the five parts of a reliable prompt: role, context, task, format, and rules.

## Why it matters for a B2B VAS team

Account managers own the client relationship. A consistent brief helps them walk into a
meeting with a regional issuer or an acquirer knowing the client's goals, which VAS
products are relevant, and what not to say.

## How to use

1. Open [prompt.md](prompt.md) and paste it into the model you use.
2. Replace the `<notes>` section with your notes. Use fictional names when practicing.
3. Compare the result with [example-output.md](example-output.md).

## What to look for in the output

- Facts and assumptions are separated.
- Missing information is marked `[UNKNOWN: ...]` instead of guessed.
- No performance claims, and nothing about other clients.
