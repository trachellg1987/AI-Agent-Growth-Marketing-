# Campaign brief

## When to use

Planning a campaign aimed at issuers, acquirers, merchants or fintechs.

## Inputs

Product, client segment, goal, budget, timing, approved proof points.

## Steps

1. Use `03-campaign-brief/prompt.md`.
2. Fill the compliance and claims-approval checklist; leave unchecked items visible.
3. Route the brief to legal/compliance before any asset is produced.

## Output

Brief with objective, audience, key message, channels, timeline, measurement, and checklist.

## Guardrails

- Regional marketing-consent rules decide who can be contacted by which channel.
- Every claim is listed with its substantiation source or flagged.
