# Webinar follow-up

## When to use

After a client webinar, to turn one event into several follow-ups.

## Inputs

Webinar summary (no attendee data in the prompt), personas attending, approved proof points.

## Steps

1. Use `06-content-repurposing/prompt.md`.
2. Account-manager talking points go to account managers, not to clients directly.
3. Emails go only to attendees with marketing consent.

## Output

Talking points, one nurture email, a short internal recap with open questions.

## Guardrails

- Do not reference what a named attendee said or asked.

## Used by

`06-content-repurposing`
