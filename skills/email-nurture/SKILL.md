# Email nurture

## When to use

Following up with contacts who engaged (webinar, workshop, content download) and have marketing consent.

## Inputs

Persona, product, the engagement that triggered the sequence, approved proof points.

## Steps

1. Three to four emails, two to three weeks apart.
2. Each email: one problem, one idea, one call to action (talk to your account manager, join a workshop).
3. Run `claims-review` and `consent-check` before scheduling.

## Output

Subject line, preview text, body (under 150 words), call to action, send timing.

## Guardrails

- No performance figures unless approved. Include the opt-out the region requires.
