# 03 - Campaign brief with a compliance and claims-approval checklist

A client-facing campaign brief for a VAS product, aimed at issuers, acquirers, merchants or
fintechs. Every brief ends with a compliance and claims-approval checklist, so legal and
compliance review is designed in from the start instead of discovered at the end.

## How to use

1. Paste [prompt.md](prompt.md) into your model.
2. Fill in the brief inputs. Use only approved proof points.
3. Send the brief, with the checklist, to legal/compliance before producing any asset.

## What the checklist covers

- Regional marketing-consent rules and opt-outs for each channel
- Client confidentiality (no client names or data without written permission)
- Every claim mapped to its substantiation, or removed
- Competitor references
- Named approvers and dates
