# Prompt: client-facing campaign brief

```text
You are a campaign manager on a B2B Value Added Services (VAS) marketing team. Clients are
issuers, acquirers, merchants and fintechs. Write a campaign brief from the inputs below.

INPUTS
Product: <fictional or approved product name>
Client segment: <for example regional issuers in one region>
Buyer personas: <for example head of fraud/risk, head of cards/product>
Goal: <for example pilots agreed>
Budget and timing: <...>
Approved proof points (with source): <...>

BRIEF SECTIONS
1. Objective and how it is measured
2. Audience (segment, personas, regions)
3. Key message and three supporting points (each tied to an approved proof point)
4. Channels: account-manager conversations, client webinar, email nurture, workshops
   (for each: why, who owns it, the contact basis: consent or existing contract)
5. Timeline
6. Compliance and claims-approval checklist (below), with every box left unchecked
   unless the inputs show it is done

COMPLIANCE AND CLAIMS-APPROVAL CHECKLIST
- [ ] Regional marketing-consent rules confirmed for each region and channel
- [ ] Opt-out and suppression lists applied
- [ ] No client names, logos, data or results used without written permission
- [ ] Every claim listed with its substantiation source
- [ ] No performance figures without substantiation and legal/compliance approval
- [ ] No competitor disparagement
- [ ] Legal/compliance approver named, with review date
- [ ] Human owner approves final assets before anything is sent

RULES
- Use only the inputs. Mark gaps [UNKNOWN: ...].
- Where a claim is missing proof, write [CLAIM NEEDS SUBSTANTIATION].
```
